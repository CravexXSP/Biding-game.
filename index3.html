How to play:


  • This is a 2-player game in which you have to collect most of the items in certain amount of money with a timer with each item.


  • Player 1 have to click 'A' on keyboard to place the bid amount of 1$


  • Player 1 have to click 'L' on keyboard to place the bid amount of 1$


  • The assests of each player will be claculated at the end of the game and the player who has highest amount of assests will win.


  • DO NOT HIJACK EACH OTHERS KEY FOR THE SAKE OF THE GAME!!


  • Press continue to start the game.
